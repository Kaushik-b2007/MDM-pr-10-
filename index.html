THE PROBLEM

Why Waste Soil Management Matters

Improper handling of excavated and waste soil can create environmental, economic and social problems.

🏗️

Construction Waste

Excavation during construction can generate large amounts of unwanted soil.

🌊

Water Pollution

Soil dumped near water bodies can increase sedimentation and pollution.

🌳

Land Degradation

Unplanned dumping can damage landscapes and reduce useful land.

💰

Economic Loss

Useful soil can become a wasted resource when it is not reused properly.

SOLUTIONS

Better Ways to Manage Waste Soil

♻️

Reuse

Reuse suitable soil for landscaping, gardening, road construction and site leveling.

🌱

Restoration

Use suitable soil to restore degraded land, gardens and green spaces.

🚛

Responsible Disposal

Contaminated or unsuitable soil should be handled and disposed of according to local regulations.
